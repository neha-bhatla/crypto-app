# CoinHub

CoinHub is a crypto dashboard where you can check prices, explore exchanges, and see what your crypto is worth.

## Market overview

See the top 100 crypto assets, their current prices, and how much they’ve changed over the past 24 hours. The overview also shows their combined market value, trading volume, and Bitcoin’s share.

Select a coin to view its price chart. You can look at the past day, week, month, six months, or year.

## Find your coins

Search for a coin by name or symbol, or sort the list by price, daily change, and market value. Tap the star beside a coin to add it to your watchlist and find it again later.

## Explore exchanges

Browse crypto exchanges and compare their trust scores and daily trading volumes. Search for a specific exchange or follow its website link to learn more.

## Crypto converter

Choose two currencies, enter an amount, and see the estimated conversion. You can convert between crypto assets and US dollars, or swap the currencies to reverse the calculation.

## On any screen

CoinHub adjusts to phones, tablets, and desktops. On smaller screens, the tables keep the main details visible and let you scroll when needed.

Prices may be delayed, and conversions don’t include fees. If fresh prices aren’t available, you’ll see a notice with saved data or an option to try again.

Market data is provided by [CoinGecko](https://www.coingecko.com/).
